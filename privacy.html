What we collect

When you contact us via email or use the MPact Asset Management Platform, we collect the information you provide (such as name, email, role) and standard server logs (IP address, browser, timestamp). The platform itself is governed by separate access agreements and an end-to-end audit log.

To respond to your inquiry, provision platform access if requested, and meet our compliance and recordkeeping obligations as an SEC-registered investment adviser. We do not sell personal information.
