MPact Capital Divisions

Five divisions, one mission: deploy capital where it compounds for people.

The Asset & Investment Management as well as the Capital Markets divisions are underpinned by our proprietary, digitally-native financial infrastructure.

MPC Asset Management

Core Housing Impact Fund (CHIF)

MPC curates customizable separately managed accounts (SMA aka "Pacts") tailored to our client's target impact-return profile. This novel approach offers optimized exposure to home price appreciation (HPA) through investments in Homium and other tokenized fixed income and liquid yielding assets.

  • Pacts are governed by a custom Investment & Impact Management Agreement (IIMA) for superior transparency, real-time monitoring & reporting, and greater control of investment and impact outcomes — with mutualism in every mandate.
MPC Investment Management

Private Credit Impact Solutions

MPC delivers place-based OCIO solutions for a variety of clients, including non-profit organizations, faith-based institutions, municipalities, developers, family offices and foundations.

  • Comprehensive impact investment services and bespoke impact loan structures to support mission-aligned banks and lenders, driving access to the ownership economy across small businesses and homeownership.
  • From management of proceeds, to origination programs and portfolio management, MPC delivers seamless, strategic OCIO solutions for sustainable, mission-driven lending and investment initiatives.
MPC Capital Markets

Capital & Liquidity. AI Securitization.

MPC supports a wide range of clients with innovative capital-market strategies that fund modernized, mission-aligned impact projects — required to cure the affordable and workforce housing crisis.

  • Capital & Liquidity Strategies — Proven solutions for traditional and tokenized structures including structured products, taxable & tax-exempt debt, loans, and equity originations.
  • AI Securitization Modeling — In-house, AI-enabled tools to securitize and rate non-conforming loan pools, providing unparalleled portfolio research while unlocking secondary liquidity options in an otherwise illiquid market.

MPact Asset Management Platform

Institutional-grade portal with auditable, SEC-aligned controls.

Purpose-built for an SEC-registered investment adviser. Staff and SMA clients monitor portfolios, impact, and reporting on infrastructure designed to meet the controls expected of registrants under Reg S-P, Reg S-ID, the SEC's cybersecurity risk-management rules, and the evolving regulatory framework for digital asset custody, safekeeping, and disclosure.

Invite-only with mandatory MFA

Every staff and client account is provisioned by an MPact relationship lead. Multi-factor authentication is enforced on enrollment — no public sign-up, no password-only access.

Encryption in transit & at rest

Industry-standard encryption for every connection and all stored data. Authentication secrets stored under envelope encryption. Hosted on SOC 2 Type II-audited infrastructure.

Append-only audit log

Timestamped record of every action and view, captured at the database layer. Built to meet SEC Rule 204-2 books-and-records retention.

MPC controls on sensitive actions

Multi-party computation gating for treasury and disbursement workflows. No single user can move material capital alone; every approval is logged.

Security & compliance posture

  • SEC-registered investment adviser — CRD #335008, SEC #801-135352
  • SOC 2 Type II-audited hosting infrastructure
  • Designed in alignment with Reg S-P, Reg S-ID, and the SEC's cybersecurity risk-management rules
  • Mandatory multi-factor authentication and industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest
  • Append-only audit log retained per SEC Rule 204-2
  • Written information-security policy (WISP) and documented incident-response plan
  • Multi-party computation gating on treasury and disbursement workflows

References reflect MPact Capital's intended security posture and the platform's underlying infrastructure. Active certifications and most-recent attestation reports are available to qualified institutional clients on request.
